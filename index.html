Four steps. You do the first one.

  1. 01

    Drop your resume

    PDF, Word or text. It's read in your browser and never uploaded to us; there is no "us" server.

  2. 02

    We search for your field

    Nursing, accounting, teaching, marketing, engineering or anything else: we read your major and past jobs, then search employers' own job boards and an all-industry feed for that kind of work.

  3. 03

    Every job gets a score

    5/5 means apply now. Below 4 we tell you why to skip it. Scores check the posting's requirements, keywords and wording against your resume. Citizenship or clearance rules are checked against your profile, never counted as resume gaps.

  4. 04

    Tailored and checked

    Your resume is reworded with the posting's own keywords and exported as a PDF. That PDF is then read back like screening software reads it and scored again, so you see exactly what they'll see.

Apply queue

Each job gets its own tailored resume PDF and answers. Open one to review it and apply, or export the whole queue to Autopilot, a free helper that opens each application and fills it in for you (setup is one command, see below).

How to run Autopilot (2 minutes, once)
  1. Install Node.js.
  2. Download this project: ReverseApply.zip, unzip it, and open a terminal in that folder.
  3. Run npm install once.
  4. Put your exported reverseapply-pack.json in that folder and run npm start. It opens each application, fills your details and uploads the tailored PDF. Add -- --submit to have it press Submit too when a form has no extra questions or CAPTCHA.
